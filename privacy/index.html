Stasis Crypt Uploader

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Stasis Crypt Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Stasis Crypt. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it. The owner uses it to upload and manage videos, thumbnails, and channel branding on their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Information the app uses

When the owner connects a Google account, the app receives OAuth tokens and the YouTube data needed to do that work. That includes the videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and channel-branding images the owner chooses to upload or change, and the channel information the YouTube Data API returns so those actions can be completed.

How that information is used

The app uses this information only to upload and manage videos, thumbnails, and channel branding on the owner’s own channel. It is not used for any other purpose.

Selling, sharing, and advertising

This information is never sold. It is not shared with third parties. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to build advertising profiles.

Tokens

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored privately by the channel owner. They are not published. The owner can revoke the app’s access at any time on the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the app cannot call the YouTube Data API until the owner grants permission again.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Stasis Crypt Uploader’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For this app, that means:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the upload and channel-management features the owner is using.
  • It is not transferred to others, except to Google when the app calls the YouTube Data API to carry out an action the owner requested, or when the law requires it.
  • It is not used to serve advertisements.
  • It is not shown to anyone else. The only person who operates the tool is the channel owner, working with their own channel.

YouTube and Google policies

Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of Google Account data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
